🪁 Kite

Kite is a lightweight, offline productivity and mood tracker built with Python 3 — no dependencies required!
<img width="857" height="594" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a37c9e97-3010-400f-8403-b68df4d1325b" />


Designed to boost focus and well-being, Kite helps you plan focus sessions (10, 20, or 30 minutes), track your mood, and gain insightful analytics into your daily, weekly, and monthly performance — all while keeping your data private and offline.
🌟 Features

    ✅ Works on most operating systems

    💾 Saves data locally in a JSON file for secure memory recall

    🧍 Personalized water and break goals based on height, weight, age, and gender

    ⏱️ Smart focus sessions with timed breaks

    😌 Mood tracking after each session

    📊 Analytics dashboard showing productivity and mood insights by day, week, and month

    🌙 End-of-day recap summarizing your progress

🧠 How It Works

    Launch Kite — no installation or internet required.

    Enter your basic details (height, weight, age, gender).

    Choose your focus session length (10, 20, or 30 minutes).

    Click Start Focus to begin your countdown.

    After your break, log your mood.

    Review productivity analytics any time under Analytics.

    End your day with a full daily recap of focus time and mood trends.

⚙️ Tech Info

    Language: Python 3

    Storage: Local JSON file

    Dependencies: None

    Mode: Fully offline

    Developed with: AI-assisted design
